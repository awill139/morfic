# Code of Conduct

We want Morfic to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to help.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive; assume good faith; give and accept feedback graciously;
focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private
information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

**Reporting:** to report a problem, use *Security → Report a vulnerability* on this repository (it
reaches the maintainers privately) and mark it as a conduct report, or contact a maintainer directly.
Reports are handled confidentially.

This applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project
in public. It is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
